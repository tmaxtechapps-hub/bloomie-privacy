Privacy Policy for Bloomie

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Versiunea în limba română

Bloomie ("the app", "we", "us") is an app for tracking habits, goals and a bucket list. It is provided by TMaxTech SRL. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

Everything you create in Bloomie stays on your device. The app has no user accounts and no servers of our own, and it does not send your habits, goals, bucket list or any other content to us or to anyone else. We do not sell your data. The app contains no analytics and no crash reporting.

The one exception is advertising: the free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects certain device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. Buying Bloomie Premium removes all ads. See "Advertising" below.

Information Stored on Your Device

The content you enter is kept in a database inside the app's private storage on your device. Depending on what you choose to record, this can include:

  • Habits, goals and bucket list items, with their categories, targets, deadlines and reminder times
  • Completion logs, streaks, milestones, notes, journal entries and budgets
  • Achievements, points, levels, and app preferences (such as language, theme and currency)

We have no access to this data and cannot see, recover or delete it for you.

Permissions the App Uses

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set for your habits. Reminders are created and scheduled on your device.
  • Alarms and reminders, run at startup: so reminders fire at the time you chose and are rescheduled after your device restarts. This work runs on your device only.
  • Photos (optional): only when you choose to save a shareable image, such as an achievement card, to your gallery. The app adds that image and does not read, upload or scan your photo library.
  • Internet: used only to load ads in the free version and to process and check purchases through Google Play. Your content is never sent over the internet by the app.
  • Google Play billing: to offer the Premium purchase.
  • Vibrate: for haptic feedback.

Backups and Sharing

  • Backup files: you can export a backup file and send it with your device's share feature. It goes only where you choose (for example cloud storage, email or a messaging app), and the receiving service's own privacy policy then applies. The file is not encrypted. When you import a backup, the app reads only the file you pick.
  • No Android cloud backup: Bloomie is excluded from Android's automatic backup, so your data is not copied to your Google account. An exported backup file is the only way to move your data to a new device or keep it after uninstalling.
  • Sharing images: when you share an image or text from the app, you choose the destination app, and that app's privacy policy applies. Bloomie does not send anything on its own.

Advertising

The free version of Bloomie shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, AdMob and its ad partners may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address and general device information. Google processes this data under its own Privacy Policy and advertising policies.

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Bloomie shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can refuse personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • Removing ads: after you buy Bloomie Premium, the app stops showing and requesting ads.

Purchases

The Premium purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. The app asks Google Play whether you own Premium so it can unlock it and restore it on a new device. We do not receive or store your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy for how payment data is processed.

Ratings

The app may ask you to rate it using Google Play's in-app review feature. The review is sent to Google Play, not to us, and Google decides whether the prompt is shown.

Your Choices and Rights

You can edit or delete any habit, goal, bucket list item or entry at any time, or erase everything with "Reset All Data" in Settings. To remove all app data, uninstall Bloomie or clear its storage in your device's app settings. Because we never hold a copy, nothing remains with us. Delete any backup files where you stored them.

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to give access to, correct or delete. For data processed by Google for advertising or purchases, you can use the controls in your Google account, the ad privacy options in the app, or contact Google. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority; in Romania this is the ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro).

Children's Privacy

Bloomie is intended for adults aged 18 and over, as set out in our Terms of Use. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the free version shows ads, it is not designed for use by children.

Changes to This Policy

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
