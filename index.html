Privacy Policy for Bloomie

Last updated: 21 July 2026

Bloomie ("the app", "we", "us") is a habit and goal tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information. Your privacy is important to us, and Bloomie is designed to keep your data private and entirely on your own device.

Summary

Bloomie does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no servers, and no analytics or tracking. Everything you create in the app stays on your device.

Information We Store on Your Device

All data you enter is stored locally on your device using an on-device database and is never sent to us or any third party. This includes:

  • Habits, goals, and bucket list items you create
  • Completion logs, streaks, notes, journal entries, and budgets
  • Achievements, points, levels, and app preferences (such as theme and language)
  • Any photos or images you attach to items (stored only on your device)

Because this data lives only on your device, it is removed when you delete the app or clear its data. We have no access to it and cannot recover it for you.

Permissions the App Uses

  • Notifications: to show local reminders you schedule for your habits and goals. These reminders are generated on your device; no reminder data leaves your device.
  • Photos / Media (optional): only used if you choose to save a shareable image (for example, an achievement card) to your device's photo gallery. The app does not read, upload, or scan your photo library.
  • Exact alarms / Boot completed: to reliably deliver scheduled reminders at the correct time, including after your device restarts.
  • Vibrate: for haptic feedback within the app.

Sharing Content

Bloomie lets you share images or content you create using your device's standard share feature. When you choose to share, you select the destination app (for example, a messaging or social app). That action is initiated entirely by you, and the receiving app's own privacy policy then applies. Bloomie does not transmit this content on its own.

Data Collection and Third Parties

Bloomie contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers. We do not use cookies. We do not collect device identifiers. No data is shared with third parties.

Children's Privacy

Bloomie does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected or transmitted, the app does not gather information from children under the age of 13.

Purchases

If Bloomie offers an in-app purchase, the transaction is handled entirely by the Google Play billing system. Bloomie does not receive or store your payment details. Please refer to Google's privacy policy for information about how payment data is processed.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
